# Hi, I'm Ashima Singh, MPH 👋

### Epidemiologist | Public Health Surveillance Analyst | Health Data Scientist

I am an epidemiologist with experience in infectious disease surveillance, overdose prevention, spatial epidemiology, health disparities research, and public health data analysis. My work focuses on transforming surveillance and research data into actionable insights that support evidence-based public health decision-making.

Currently, I serve as a **Biological Scientist III at the Florida Department of Health – Broward County**, where I lead monthly and quarterly Overdose Fatality Reviews under Florida's Data to Action initiative. My work includes abstracting and analyzing 450+ medical records per review cycle, identifying polysubstance use patterns, evaluating mortality trends, and supporting county-level overdose prevention strategies.
