# Hi, I'm Ashima Singh, MPH 👋

### Epidemiologist | Public Health Surveillance Analyst | Health Data Scientist

I am an epidemiologist with experience in infectious disease surveillance, overdose prevention, spatial epidemiology, health disparities research, public health informatics, and health data analytics. My work focuses on transforming surveillance and research data into actionable insights that support evidence-based public health decision-making.

Currently, I serve as a **Biological Scientist III at the Florida Department of Health – Broward County**, where I contribute to multiple initiatives under Florida's **Overdose Data to Action (OD2A)** program. My work includes leading monthly and quarterly Overdose Fatality Reviews, abstracting and analyzing overdose-related medical records, conducting surveillance of emerging drug compounds, managing REDCap databases, developing R-based data visualizations, evaluating mortality and substance use trends, and supporting county-level overdose prevention and harm reduction strategies.
